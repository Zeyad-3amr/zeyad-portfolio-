About

Why engineering → product?

Not a career pivot. A natural progression.

I started as a backend engineer because I love building things. But I kept asking questions that weren't in my job description — why are we building this? Who actually needs it? What problem does it solve?

That curiosity led me to study product management formally — PM courses from the University of Maryland, a Technical PM course on LinkedIn Learning, and an AI for PMs workshop with Dorra Mlouhi. More importantly, I stopped thinking about building products and actually built one.

Rekur is a digital loyalty platform I took from a personal frustration — forgetting my stamp card at coffee shops — to a live deployed product currently in pilot discussions with a Cairo coffee shop. End-to-end: discovery, PRD, product decisions, deployment.

I'm looking for a role where engineering depth is a feature, not a footnote — where I can sit with engineers as a peer and translate constraints into product decisions that actually make sense.
