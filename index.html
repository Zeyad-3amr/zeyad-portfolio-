About

Why engineering → product?

Not a career pivot. A natural progression.

I started as a backend engineer because I love building things. But I kept asking questions that weren't in my job description — why are we building this? Who actually needs it? What problem does it solve?

That curiosity led me to study product management formally — PM courses from the University of Maryland, a Technical PM course on LinkedIn Learning, and an AI for PMs workshop with Dorra Mlouhi. More importantly, I stopped thinking about building products and actually built one.

Rekur is a digital loyalty platform I took from a personal frustration — forgetting my stamp card at coffee shops — to a live, deployed product. End-to-end: discovery, PRD, product decisions, threat model, and deployment. Now pre-pilot — built and live, not yet launched with a shop.

I also run Agile in practice, not just in theory — I led daily standups for my squad before a dedicated Product Owner joined, and work through sprint planning, backlog refinement, and retrospectives.

I'm looking for a role where engineering depth is a feature, not a footnote — where I can sit with engineers as a peer and translate constraints into product decisions that actually make sense.

⭐ Featured — Full PM cycle

Rekur — Digital Coffee Loyalty Platform

Identified a market gap in Egypt through personal experience and field observation of how coffee shops run paper stamp cards. Wrote a full PRD across 5 versioned iterations. Built and deployed the MVP with production-grade security. Now pre-pilot — live, not yet launched with a shop.

Product decisions made
  • Identified market gap — no digital loyalty solution existed in Egypt's coffee market
  • Wrote full PRD: user flows, security model, success metrics, 5 versioned iterations
  • Removed cashier from digital flow entirely — friction eliminated at point of purchase
  • Defined a 7-minute cooldown window sized to worst-case coffee-shop queue times
  • Scoped MVP deliberately — cut OTP, native app, and white-label features for v1
  • Mapped the funnel to AARRR, made retention the north-star metric, and designed an SMS "almost there" reminder as a first A/B test
Security architecture shipped
  • HMAC-SHA256 signed rotating QR codes refreshing every 20 seconds
  • 120-second session expiry — saved links from home blocked automatically
  • 7-minute server-side cooldown per phone number — two-layer enforcement
  • Animated reward screen — proves reward is live, not a screenshot
  • Atomic DB transactions on every stamp — no double-count, no lost stamp
  • Apple Wallet passes with live APNs push updates as stamps are added
  • Admin dashboard: real-time data, manual stamp fallback, logo upload
